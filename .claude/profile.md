## 3. About the Hospital

Ogunsusi Specialist Hospital is a privately owned specialist healthcare facility located on Zion Estate, Temidire Street, in Ile‑Oluji — the headquarters town of the Ile‑Oluji/Okeigbo Local Government Area of Ondo State, Nigeria. The hospital serves patients from Ile‑Oluji and neighbouring towns and villages across central Ondo State.

The facility is officially recognised in the **Ondo State register of coded health facilities** as a specialist hospital, and is an **accredited provider on private health-insurance (HMO) networks** — allowing insured patients to access treatment on a cashless basis. It is listed as a **Category "A"** facility on approved-hospital schedules.

The hospital operates a complete private-hospital service line: ambulatory (walk-in) outpatient care, ward admissions with round-the-clock nursing, an operating theatre for surgical procedures, and mortuary and embalming services for the community.

## 4. Services

### 🩺 General Outpatient Services (GOPD)
Walk-in consultations, diagnosis, and treatment of general medical conditions. Includes routine medical check-ups, treatment of common illnesses and minor ailments, follow-up reviews, health advice, and onward referrals where specialist attention is required.

### 🛏️ Inpatient (Admission) Services
Ward admission with 24-hour nursing care for patients who require close monitoring, ongoing treatment, or recovery under medical supervision. Suitable for patients recovering from illness, undergoing extended treatment, or convalescing after surgery.

### 🔪 Surgical Services
Operative procedures carried out on-site in the hospital's theatre, covering general surgical care — both elective (planned) and emergency procedures — as clinically indicated, with pre-operative assessment and post-operative recovery care.

### ⚰️ Mortuary & Embalming Services
Professional morgue facilities offering body preservation, embalming, and dignified mortuary services for bereaved families and the wider community.
